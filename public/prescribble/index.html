Ideating Other Solutions

Two alternative concepts were explored and rejected before landing on the selection-based approach. Both are detailed below.

Fully Typed Digital Prescriptions

Rejected: interviews suggested doctors prefer speed, and this solution assumes a 15 min consultation window.

See pros & cons
Pros and cons of fully typed digital prescriptions
Pros Cons
  • Very Clear prescriptions
  • Easy DB integration
  • Standardised format
  • Typing speed <<< writing
  • Disrupts existing workflow
  • Resistance from doctors

Voice-Assisted Prescription System

Rejected: speech recognition struggles with background clinic noise, and doctors don't want to dictate sensitive information aloud.

See pros & cons
Pros and cons of a voice-assisted prescription system
Pros Cons
  • Faster than typing
  • Hands-free interaction
  • Structures Rx automatically
  • Background noise in clinics
  • Accent recognition issues
  • Privacy concerns in crowd
