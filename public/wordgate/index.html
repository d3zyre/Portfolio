My task: redesign this homescreen so Play Solo and Host Game are the primary and secondary CTAs.

What I was given, and what I assumed was wrong with it.

What came out of it: Play Solo and Host Game up front, the rest in a carousel.

  • 2 CTAs at the focal centrePlay Solo and Host Game first
  • Carousel for the restEverything else, one swipe away
  • Share moved top leftGrouped with settings
  • Coin counter addedVersion 1 had no currency bar

Then added colour. The founder liked it enough to make it the base for the real homescreen.

Once I joined and learnt the game, another layer appeared: competitive game modes and an exploratory one, clashing on the same screen.

How might we

Give each game mode its own space on the homescreen, and still keep Play Solo and Host Game as the primary CTAs?

Players liked scrolling modes sideways, but the menu below already scrolled sideways too.

Static layouts either lost the two CTAs or felt empty without ads.

Competitive modes as tabs on top kept the exploratory mode separate, in the menu below.

Final: three equal shortcuts below, because each drives revenue.

Trade-off: the exploratory mode lost its spotlight.
